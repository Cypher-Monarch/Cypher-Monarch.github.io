Under the hood.
And then a little deeper.

I’m Cypher-Monarch. I make stuff that lives somewhere between software, systems, and UX.

Most things start with a simple idea and somehow become considerably more complicated than they needed to be. I like Linux, good interfaces, quiet systems, and software that gets out of the way once it has done its job. I also tend to over-engineer things. Sometimes that’s a problem. Usually it’s the fun part.
